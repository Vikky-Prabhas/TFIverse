India Box Office Market Report

India's theatrical market continues to witness strong activity with 192 tracked films generating approximately ₹76.49 crore in gross collections from 31.93 lakh ticket sales. BFILMY is currently monitoring 37,622 shows across 1,321 cities and 34 states.

Telugu currently leads all language markets with 11.38 lakh tickets sold and ₹28.99 crore in tracked gross collections. Hindi follows as the second-largest language segment, while Tamil continues to maintain a strong theatrical footprint.

The widest release in the country today is Peddi with 9,875 tracked shows. Meanwhile, Peddi emerges as the highest grossing film in BFILMY tracking.

Average occupancy currently stands at 35.3%, while the nationwide average ticket price is approximately ₹239.53. These metrics indicate overall audience demand levels and cinema utilisation across the country.

Among regional markets, Andhra Pradesh continues to be the strongest contributing state, while Hyderabad remains the most active city based on tracked ticket sales.

A total of 12,081 venues and more than 90,46,296 available seats are currently being monitored by BFILMY's nationwide tracking system. The platform is actively tracking 192 movies across 26 languages, providing one of the most comprehensive real-time views of India's theatrical market.

BFILMY's live tracking system updates throughout the day using real-time ticket sales, occupancy trends, gross estimates and show-count monitoring from cinema locations across India.

State-Wise Market Analysis

Andhra Pradesh currently stands as India's strongest theatrical state with approximately 6.11 lakh admissions and nearly ₹13.75 crore in tracked collections.

Tamil Nadu continues to deliver substantial box office activity, while Telangana maintains one of the healthiest regional theatrical markets in the country.

Together, Andhra Pradesh, Tamil Nadu, Telangana, Maharashtra and Karnataka represent the core of India's theatrical economy and contribute a major share of nationwide ticket sales.

City-Level Performance Trends

Hyderabad currently ranks as India's highest-contributing city by tracked ticket sales with more than 3,11,870 admissions and approximately ₹9.25 crore in gross collections.

Bengaluru, Chennai and Mumbai continue to rank among the strongest urban theatrical markets, while Pune maintains significant contribution to regional box office activity.

The continued dominance of major metropolitan centres highlights the importance of urban moviegoing audiences in shaping nationwide theatrical performance trends.

Top Language Markets Today

Telugu currently leads the Indian theatrical market with approximately 11.38 lakh admissions and nearly ₹28.99 crore in tracked gross collections. While Hindi cinema operates on a significantly wider theatrical footprint, Telugu releases are generating stronger audience concentration with occupancy levels touching 55.21%.

Hindi remains India's largest language market by show count with 16,800 tracked shows, generating ₹17.69 crore and maintaining an average ticket price of ₹259.85.

Tamil, English and Marathi continue to contribute significantly to nationwide admissions, highlighting the diversity of theatrical demand across regional industries. Together, the five largest language markets account for the overwhelming majority of tracked ticket sales across India today.

About BFILMY Live Box Office Tracking

BFILMY provides real-time movie tracking across India by analysing ticket sales, occupancy, gross collections, show counts and audience trends from cinema chains and independent theatres. The platform covers Hindi, Telugu, Tamil, Malayalam, Kannada, Punjabi, Bengali, Bhojpuri and several regional film industries. Users can explore city-wise, state-wise, chain-wise and language-wise box office performance along with advance booking trends and occupancy reports. Data is continuously updated throughout the day, helping movie enthusiasts, trade followers and industry observers understand box office performance as it develops across India.
