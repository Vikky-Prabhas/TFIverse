Colour is status, never decoration. If a number is orange it is because it went up. If a badge is red it is because the film lost money. Nothing is tinted to look nice — so when colour appears, it means something.

Contrast, measured: bone 15.2:1 · gold 7.3:1 · marigold 7.1:1 · bone-faint 5.1:1, all on lac. Deep kumkum is 2.6:1 so it never carries text on dark — it is a fill with bone on top (5.8:1) or a stamp border, and kumkum-lit (5.6:1) does the falling-number text instead.

06 the rules, so the site stays one thing
Radius means touchable

0px on data surfaces and ledger rows. 4px on anything you can click. 2px on posters. Round only on avatars. Right now everything is rounded-2xl, so radius tells you nothing.

Depth by tone, not by alpha

Surfaces step lac-deep → lac → lac-raise. No more stacking white/[0.03] on white/[0.02], which is why the current page looks flat in bright light and muddy in the dark.

Three section weights

160px for the one marquee, 96px for standard sections, 48px for scroll rails that bleed off the right edge. Nine sections at identical py-8 is why nothing reads as important.

One orchestrated motion

Keep the 222-frame scroll sequence — it is the best thing on the site. Then delete every per-section whileInView fade and every card hover-lift. Motion after that only answers a tap.
